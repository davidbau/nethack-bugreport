hidden monsters are unhidden whenever their level is reloaded

Commit 055caaffb moved find_lev_obj() in getlev() to after the monster
loop, so that it follows the loop's set_residency() calls.  But the
same loop calls hideunder() and hide_monst() for monsters that hide
under objects, and they look at svl.level.objects[][], which is still
empty there.  hideunder() finds no object and clears mundetected, so a
snake hiding under an object is out in the open after changing levels
and coming back, after save and restore, and on bones levels.

Call set_residency() for shopkeepers in a loop of its own, then
find_lev_obj(), then the monster loop.  find_lev_obj() still follows
set_residency(), and the objects are on the map again when the hiding
code runs.
