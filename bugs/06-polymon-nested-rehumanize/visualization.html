Why the existing recursion guard does not catch it

retouch_equipment() opens with if (!nesting++) clear_bypasses();, which works when the two calls nest. Here they are sequential: rehumanize() ran its own retouch_equipment(2) (polyself.c:1415) and returned, decrementing nesting back to 0, so polymon()'s call at polyself.c:1021 clears the bypass bits again and re-scans all of gi.invent.
