Why the patched run survives

The hero does not die of lava damage — a level-30 Valkyrie absorbs d(6,6) indefinitely. They die because the game removed their water walking boots while they stood in lava, using a newt's body plan to decide what a human can wear. Without the boots, lava_effects() skips losehp() entirely and goes straight to u.uhp = -1; done(BURNING). The guard if (gy.youmonst.data != uptr) return; stops break_armor() at the revert; the boots stay on, Wwalking stays true, and every later lava_effects() keeps taking the survivable branch.

Note: uptr is stale but not dangling — gy.youmonst.data always points into the static mons[] table. This is a logic error, not memory unsafety.
